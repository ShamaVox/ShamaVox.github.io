Proof

A multi-agent platform that put retailers' products on sale the same day.

As cofounder and CTO of an AI-native wholesale platform, I designed and built the system and took it from proof of concept to paying customers. Supplier catalogs arrived in every format, and retailers waited days for listings. After launch, listings went live the moment a purchase order arrived.

Getting a model to read a catalog was the easy part. Making every field correct on data nobody had seen before was the work. So I built the checking before the features: a golden set, field-level evals, confidence scoring, and specialized agents coordinated by deterministic code.

IngestionAny supplier catalog, any layout.
Specialized agentsExtract each field with a confidence score.
Trust layerValidate, repair safely, route uncertainty to a human.
DeliveryPublish into the retailer's store.
Correction loop: every retailer fix fed the review queue and the eval set, so the system got better with use.
